Here is a realistic, commercially honest 90-day plan designed for your specific constraints (10 hours/week, €700 budget, no ads, no hype).

### 1. Positioning: The "Human AI" Editor
You are not teaching "AI Content Creation." You are teaching **"Expertise Amplification."**

- **The Problem:** Your audience (Designers, Copywriters, Consultants) hates marketing because it feels like a second job. They fear AI will make them sound like everyone else.
- **Your Position:** "I help independent experts in the EU turn their hard-won expertise into consistent, non-cringe marketing assets using AI as a *junior assistant*, not a replacement."
- **The Vibe:** Practical, "do this, not that," culturally aware of the EU market (slightly more skeptical of hype than the US market).

### 2. The First Niche: Independent Copywriters & Content Strategists
**Why them?**
1.  **The "Hairdresser's Haircut" Problem:** They write for clients all day but have no time to write for themselves. They feel the most shame about an empty LinkedIn profile.
2.  **High AI Anxiety:** They are the most afraid of AI replacing them, so they are highly motivated to learn how to *use* it to stay relevant.
3.  **Language Fit:** They understand nuance. They will appreciate your "don't sound generic" angle more than a pure consultant might.

### 3. The Offer Ladder (The Revenue Path)
To hit €3k/month, you need an average of ~15-20 customers per month across these tiers.

- **Tier 1: The "De-Genericizer" Template Pack (€29)**
    - *What it is:* Not just prompts. A Notion/Swipe File system. 10 "Human Voice" prompt chains, 5 LinkedIn post templates (with "boring vs. brilliant" examples), and a 30-minute "AI Editing Checklist."
    - *Goal:* Low friction entry point. Converts followers to customers.
- **Tier 2: The "90-Minute Content Sprint" Live Cohort (€149)**
    - *What it is:* A 2-hour live Zoom workshop (max 15 people). You guide them through setting up their AI "Voice Vault" and they leave with 2 weeks of content scheduled.
    - *Goal:* Cash flow and community building. Run this in Month 2 and Month 3.
- **Tier 3: The "Authority Engine" Implementation (€750 - €1,200)**
    - *What it is:* Done-with-you. You spend 2 weeks with a client. You interview them, build their entire AI prompt library, set up their content system, and train them for 60 minutes.
    - *Goal:* One or two of these per month gets you to your target.

### 4. The 90-Day Plan (10 Hours/Week)

**Budget Allocation (€700):**
- **€300:** High-quality microphone (e.g., Shure MV7 or Rode PodMic) – *essential for YouTube/Video.*

    3.  **Run Cohort #2.**

### 5. The Repeatable Content Series: "The 20-Minute Audit"
This is your flagship series. It works on LinkedIn, Email, and YouTube.

- **The Concept:** Every week, you take a piece of generic AI content (or a boring LinkedIn post from a real profile—anonymized) and you "fix" it.
- **The Structure:**
    1.  **The Diagnosis:** "Here is why this post sounds like a robot." (Point out the lack of specific nouns, the passive voice, the listicle structure).
    2.  **The Prompt:** "Here is the exact prompt I used to fix it." (Show the prompt chain).
    3.  **The Result:** "Here is the humanized version."
- **Why it works:** It proves your expertise immediately. It is inherently shareable. It demonstrates the *process*, not just the result.

### 6. Metrics That Matter (The Dashboard)
Do not look at vanity metrics. Track these weekly:

1.  **The "Click-Through" Rate (CTR) on LinkedIn:** Are people clicking "see more"? (Target: >4%).
2.  **Email Reply Rate:** When you send your newsletter, how many people reply? (Target: >5%). This is the strongest buying signal.
3.  **Lead Magnet Conversion:** How many profile views turn into email subscribers? (Target: >10%).
4.  **Revenue per Follower:** Total revenue divided by followers. (This tells you if your audience is qualified).
5.  **DM to Call Conversion:** How many DMs does it take to book a sales call?

### 7. Weekly Review Rhythm (30 Minutes, Friday)
- **10 Min - Data:** Check the metrics above. Which post had the highest "dwell time" or replies?
- **10 Min - Qualitative:** Read the comments. What specific words did people use to describe their pain? ("I feel like a fraud," "I don't have time," "I sound like a brochure"). Write these down. Use them in your next week's copy.
- **10 Min - The "Kill or Double" Decision:** Look at your content. If a topic flopped, kill it. If a topic did well, double down on it next week (make a video about it, write an email about it).

### 8. The First Three Experiments

**Experiment 1: The "Voice Vault" Lead Magnet (Weeks 1-4)**
- **Hypothesis:** Copywriters are more likely to give their email for a *system* (Prompt Chain) than for a *PDF* (Ebook).
- **Method:** Put the link in your LinkedIn featured section and at the end of every post.
- **Success Metric:** 50 new email subscribers by Day 30.

**Experiment 2: The "Anti-Hype" Hook (Weeks 3-6)**
- **Hypothesis:** Your audience is sick of "10x your output" posts. They respond better to negative framing or "constraints."
- **Method:** Write two posts with identical content but different hooks.
    - *Hook A (Hype):* "How to 10x your content output with AI."
    - *Hook B (Anti-Hype):* "Why using AI to write your LinkedIn posts is making you look cheap."
- **Success Metric:** Hook B gets 2x the comments of Hook A.

**Experiment 3: The "Live Teardown" Webinar (Weeks 6-8)**
- **Hypothesis:** A live, interactive workshop (where you fix *their* content live) converts better than a pre-recorded pitch.
- **Method:** Host a free 45-minute Zoom call. "Bring your worst AI-generated paragraph, I'll fix it live."
- **Success Metric:** 30% of attendees book a call for the €750 service or buy the €29 pack.
