# The Plan: AI-Assisted Content for Independent Creatives → €3,000/mo

## 1. Positioning (one sentence, use everywhere)

**"I help independent designers, copywriters, and consultants in Spain and Portugal use AI as a drafting tool — not a ghostwriter — to turn work they've already done into content that brings in clients, without sounding like everyone else's LinkedIn feed."**

Your wedge isn't "AI for marketing." Everyone says that. Your wedge is **"AI drafts, you decide"** — a documented process, not a magic prompt. That's defensible and matches your no-fake-urgency, no-replace-the-professional values. Say explicitly, often: *"AI won't replace your judgment. It removes the blank page."*

## 2. Pick ONE niche first: independent designers (brand, product, UX/UI)

Not "freelancers in general." Reasons:
- Designers have the richest raw material (case studies, before/afters, process shots) but the least confidence writing about it.
- "LinkedIn for copywriters" is oversaturated; "LinkedIn for designers" barely exists.
- You're in Barcelona — there's a real, walkable design community you can meet in person for free, which copywriters/consultants don't give you as easily.
- Once the system works for designers, it ports to copywriters/consultants in month 4+ almost unchanged.

You don't have to reject other followers — just make designers the example in 80% of your content for the next 90 days.

## 3. Offer Ladder (with honest math)

| Offer | Price | Format | Target volume/mo by day 90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Template pack** — "El Borrador Real" prompt + doc system | €39 | Self-serve (Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy) | 15 sales = €585 |
| **Live cohort** — "Consistent, Not Generic" | €297 | 4 weeks, 8–10 people, 2 live calls/week + async | 1 cohort of 8 every 6 weeks = ~€2,000/mo averaged |
| **Premium implementation** | €1,200–1,800 | 1:1, 4 weeks, build their content system + one digital product | 1 client every 6–8 weeks |

**Realistic month-by-month revenue** (not linear — be honest with yourself):
- Month 1: €0–200 (audience + validation, maybe a few template pre-sales)
- Month 2: €600–1,200 (template live + first small cohort or cohort waitlist)
- Month 3: €1,800–2,800 (cohort + first premium client)
- **€3,000/mo as a stable *average* is realistic by month 4–5, not guaranteed by day 90.** The premium offer is what actually gets you there — one €1,500 client is worth 40 template sales. Don't over-invest time in the €39 product; it's a trust-builder and list-grower, not your income engine.

## 4. 90-Day Plan (10 hrs/week)

**Phase 1 – Weeks 1–3: Validate, don't build**
- Publish the content series (below) 3x/week on LinkedIn.
- Post a simple landing page (Carrd, €0–19) describing the template pack and cohort — "coming soon, join waitlist."
- DM 5 designers/week you already know or follow — ask what their content struggle actually is. Use their language in your posts.
- Goal: 50 waitlist emails, 10 real conversations.

**Phase 2 – Weeks 4–6: Launch the cheap thing, pre-sell the expensive thing**
- Build and launch the €39 template pack (build time: ~6 hrs total, reuse your own process).
- Pre-sell the cohort at a founding price (€197 instead of €297) to your waitlist — cap at 8 seats, no fake countdown, just "8 seats, first come."
- Run a free 45-min live workshop (LinkedIn + YouTube live) as the pitch mechanism for the cohort.
- Goal: €500+ in template sales, cohort filled or half-filled.

**Phase 3 – Weeks 7–9: Run the cohort, harvest testimonials**
- Deliver the cohort (this is your 10 hrs/week ceiling — protect it, pause new content experiments this month).
- Identify 1–2 standout cohort members as premium candidates — pitch the implementation offer directly, 1:1, no funnel needed.
- Publish weekly "what we're building in the cohort" recaps (with permission) as content — this doubles as marketing for cohort #2.

**Phase 4 – Weeks 10–13: Sell premium, plan cohort #2**
- Close 1 premium client from cohort alumni or DMs.
- Open waitlist for cohort #2 at full €297.
- Review 90 days of data (below) and decide: keep designer niche, or widen to copywriters/consultants with the same system.

## 5. Repeatable Content Series: "El Borrador Real / The Real Draft"

One format, three platforms, every week:

- **LinkedIn (2x/week):** Screenshot-style post showing (1) the raw AI draft from a real prompt, (2) the edited human version, (3) one sentence on *why* you changed what you changed. This is your "not generic" proof, week after week.
- **Newsletter (1x/week):** Take the LinkedIn example and go deeper — the actual prompt used, the voice-capture question that made it personal, one reader's before/after if you have permission.
- **YouTube (1x every 2 weeks, 6–8 min):** Screen recording of the live process — you drafting with AI, then editing on camera. Low production, high trust. This is your best premium-offer sales tool; link it when pitching 1:1.

Bilingual note: alternate primary language weekly (EN one week, ES the next), always subtitle/caption the other. Don't fully duplicate — Portugal audience reads Spanish and English fine, don't overbuild a third language track yet.

## 6. Metrics (track weekly in one spreadsheet, not a dashboard tool)

- LinkedIn: post engagement rate (not follower count), DMs started by strangers
- Email: list growth, open rate, click rate on offer links
- Waitlist → paid conversion rate (this tells you if positioning is working)
- Template sales, cohort seats filled, premium calls booked → closed
- Time spent per activity (protect the 10 hrs — cut whatever isn't producing DMs or sales)

## 7. Weekly Review Rhythm (30 min, same slot every week)

Every Friday:
1. What number moved this week, and why? (Not "how many likes" — "how many DMs/replies.")
2. What's the single post/email that outperformed? Do more of that exact angle next week.
3. What did I spend time on that produced zero commercial signal? Cut it.
4. One sentence: what am I testing next week?

## 8. First Three Experiments (run in weeks 1–4)

1. **Niche framing test:** Run one week of posts explicitly for designers, one week explicitly for copywriters (same underlying template/process). Compare DMs and saves, not likes. Confirms your niche bet before you build anything.
2. **Pre-sell before build:** Sell the €39 template pack as "available in 10 days" before it's finished. If fewer than 10 people buy from a list of 300+, the offer or price is wrong — fix before building.
3. **Live workshop as sales mechanism:** Run one free 45-min workshop and pitch the cohort cold at the end (no urgency tricks — just "8 seats, here's how to join"). Your close rate here tells you whether your positioning actually converts to paid, which no amount of content volume will tell you.

## Budget (€700 / 90 days)

- €150 — Lemon Squeezy/Gumroad + email tool upgrade (Beehiiv/ConvertKit paid tier once >500 subs)
- €150 — Canva Pro + simple landing page tool + Loom/screen recording
- €200 — One in-person Barcelona meetup: rent a small room or buy coffee for 10–15 local designers. This is your highest-ROI spend — warm premium-client relationships don't come from posts.
- €100 — Spanish/Portuguese copy polishing (yourself or cheap freelancer) for your two most important pages: landing page and cohort sales page
- €100 — buffer

## One honest note

The template pack will feel like progress because it's easy to sell in small numbers, but it will not get you to €3,000/month on its own — the math doesn't work at 10 hrs/week. Your actual path to €3,000 is: **content builds trust → trust fills the cohort → cohort produces 1–2 premium clients per cycle.** Spend your energy proving that chain works, not on making the template pack perfect.
