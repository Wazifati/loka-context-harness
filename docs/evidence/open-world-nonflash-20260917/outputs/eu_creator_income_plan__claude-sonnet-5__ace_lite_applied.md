# Applied Context

- **Objective:** Build a realistic, non-generic path from current audience (1,800 LinkedIn / 320 email) toward €3,000/month via a template product, cohort, and premium offer — without ads or inflated claims.
- **Audience:** Independent designers, copywriters, and consultants in Spain, Portugal, and the EU who are strong at their craft but inconsistent at marketing it.
- **Approach:** Niche down for the first 90 days, build one repeatable cross-channel content series, validate offers with real money before scaling, review weekly.
- **Constraints:** €700 budget, 10 hours/week, no paid ads, no fake urgency, no copied content, no bought engagement, no "AI replaces professionals" claims.
- **Deliverable:** Positioning, niche choice, offer ladder, 90-day phased plan, content series format, metrics, weekly rhythm, and three concrete experiments to run immediately.
- **Success check:** Plan is fit for purpose if every recommendation is executable within 10 hrs/week and €700, tied to a real metric, and honest about how far €1,800/€320-follower audiences realistically get in 90 days.

---

## 1. Positioning

**Core line (bilingual-ready):**
*"I help independent experts turn what they already know into content and products people actually pay for — using AI as a drafting tool, not a substitute for their judgment."*

Your differentiator isn't "AI content" — everyone claims that. It's **specificity + responsibility**: you show the real workflow (prompt, edit, expert judgment applied), not a magic output. That's what will make your content feel non-generic and is also your best defense against the "AI slop" fatigue your audience already feels.

## 2. First niche to focus on (pick one, not three)

Recommendation: **independent B2B consultants and advisors** (strategy, ops, HR, marketing consultants) billing project or retainer work, not the full designer/copywriter/consultant mix.

Why this one first:
- They have the highest ability to pay for a premium implementation offer (they already sell €2–10k engagements, so €1,200–2,500 for their own marketing system isn't a stretch).
- Their core problem — "good at the work, invisible on LinkedIn" — is sharp and easy to write content around.
- Designers and copywriters remain a secondary audience for the low-cost template product and cohort; they don't disappear, they just aren't the primary content subject for 90 days.

**Assumption to validate:** that consultants in your existing audience will actually pay premium prices for this vs. treating it as "nice to have." Test this in Week 1–2 (see Experiment 1).

## 3. Offer ladder

| Tier | Offer | Price | Delivery effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | "Non-Generic AI Content System" — templates + prompt library + example workflows (bilingual EN/ES) | €29–39 | Built once, sold via Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy |
| Mid | "AI-Assisted Content Sprint" — 4-week small cohort (8–12 people), live sessions + peer review | €249–299/seat | ~4–5 hrs/week during cohort weeks |
| Premium | "Content & Offer System, Done With You" — 1:1 or 2-person intensive, 3–4 weeks, builds their voice, series, and lead offer | €1,200–2,500 | ~4–6 hrs/week per client |

**Rough (illustrative, not promised) monthly math at target run rate:**
1 premium client (€1,500) + 1 cohort seat-average (~€1,200/month amortized) + template sales (~€585 from ~15 units) ≈ **€3,285/month**. That means you need roughly *one* premium client per month and *one* cohort every 6–8 weeks, not a large volume — but this is a Month 4–6 target, not Day 90. Be explicit with yourself that hitting full €3k *inside* 90 days from this audience size is optimistic; the realistic 90-day outcome is a working engine plus first real revenue (rough range €800–2,000), with €3k/month plausible by month 4–6 if conversion assumptions hold.

## 4. 90-Day Plan

**Days 1–30 — Validate and build**
- Finalize niche positioning; audit last 20 LinkedIn posts against it, rewrite bio/headline.
- Run Experiments 1 and 3 (below) in week 1–2.
- Build the entry-tier template product using validated demand.
- Launch the repeatable content series (see below), 3x/week.
- Open a cohort waitlist; no cohort runs yet.

**Days 31–60 — First real transactions**
- Launch the template product publicly (email + LinkedIn, no ads).
- Run cohort #1 at a discounted "founding" price with 6–8 people in exchange for testimonials/case studies.
- Offer premium tier to 2–3 warm leads at a pilot rate (e.g., €900–1,000) explicitly framed as limited early-access pricing for case study rights — not fake urgency, real trade for feedback.
- Keep publishing the content series; start repurposing cohort/premium work (with permission) as case-study content.

**Days 61–90 — Standardize and price up**
- Run cohort #2 at full price using proof from cohort #1.
- Sell premium tier at full price using pilot case studies.
- Review template product pricing/positioning based on 60 days of data.
- Document the whole system (content → template → cohort → premium) so weeks 91+ run on rhythm, not reinvention.

## 5. One repeatable content series

**"The Unglamorous AI Workflow"** — a weekly case-study format, same skeleton every time: *Problem → Generic AI mistake → Where their expertise had to step in → Refined output → Lesson.*

- **Monday (LinkedIn):** short version — before/after snippet of a real workflow (yours or a client's, anonymized if needed).
- **Wednesday (Newsletter, EN/ES):** long version — the actual prompts, the edits, why the raw AI output was wrong or generic, and what expert judgment fixed it.
- **Friday (YouTube/Reels, under 3 min):** screen-recorded version showing the workflow live.

This gives you one research/writing effort producing three deliverables, and it doubles as ongoing proof for your positioning and future case studies for the cohort/premium tiers.

## 6. Metrics to track weekly

- LinkedIn: post engagement rate + saves (saves indicate "useful," which matters more than likes for your audience)
- Email: subscriber growth, open rate, click rate on series emails
- Template product: page views → purchases (conversion %)
- Cohort: waitlist size → paid conversion %
- Premium: DM/warm conversations → discovery calls booked → proposals sent → closed
- Time: hours spent per offer vs. your 10-hour weekly ceiling

## 7. Weekly review rhythm

45–60 minutes, same day each week (e.g., Sunday evening or Friday close):
1. Pull the six metrics above into one simple tracker (spreadsheet is enough).
2. Note which specific piece of content drove the most saves/replies/signups — feed that topic pattern into next week.
3. Check pipeline: how many people are in each stage (aware → subscribed → interested → paying)?
4. Check hours actually spent vs. 10-hour budget; cut or defer anything not tied to a metric that moved.
5. Write one sentence: "This week's biggest evidence-based decision is ___." Forces you to act on data, not vibes.

## 8. First three experiments (run in Week 1–2)

1. **Niche/price validation:** DM 15–20 people who fit the consultant niche with a direct, honest offer at your intended premium price. Goal isn't to close sales yet — it's to see how many take a call. Low response = revisit niche or price before building further.
2. **Content format test:** Run "Unglamorous AI Workflow" for 3 weeks in two structural variants (text-case-study vs. short screen-recording clip) and track which drives more saves/comments/email signups per post — not just likes.
3. **Pre-sell the template:** Sell the entry-tier product before it's fully built — waitlist page + early-bird price (~€19) to 20–30 people from your list. If fewer than ~10 pre-orders after a real push, treat that as a signal to adjust the product angle before investing more build time.

**Note on honesty of the plan:** none of these numbers are guarantees — they're planning assumptions based on typical conversion ranges for creator-led offers at this audience size. Treat months 1–3 as instrumented experiments; recalibrate the €3k timeline based on what Experiments 1–3 actually show in the first two weeks.
