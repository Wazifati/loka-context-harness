# Loka Root

Start here. Loka is a family of context-engineered frameworks, not one combined
prompt. Choose the first matching route and load only that framework.

1. Vague idea, research, strategy, content, or workflow design: **Loka ACE**.
2. Planning, prompt architecture, or audit without repository tools: **LokaV10**.
3. Repository, CLI, IDE, files, or coding tools: **LokaV11**.
4. A V11 mission needing periodic human review: add **LokaV12 Supervised**.
5. A bounded delegated V11 mission: add **LokaV12 Autonomous Edition**.
6. A visual builder or vibe-coding environment: **LokaV13**; move to V11 after export.

For repository work, load **Loka Guard** first. Use **Loka Build** after the
task is understood and before implementation. Use **Loka Failures** only as a
relevant risk lens, not as a competing workflow.

Each major route has a Lite form for a compact, copy-paste session, including
the new V12 Autonomous Edition Lite. Lite is an alternative to its matching Full
form, not a second framework to combine.

Do not mix V10, V11, and V13. V12 is the sole designed overlay and its two
editions are alternatives. Host platform policies and the user's current
instructions always take precedence over Loka.

See `LOKA_INDEX.md` for the complete Full, Lite, and overlay mapping.
