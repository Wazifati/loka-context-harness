# Research note: optional evaluation materials

This repository's primary purpose is a context-engineering harness, not a
model benchmark. The original prompt/rubric/runner materials remain as an
optional research artefact and are not required for installation or normal use.

Any future evaluation should begin with a new, preregistered methodology. At a
minimum it needs a clear control condition, exact model/configuration snapshots,
hard spend limits, resumable checkpoints, recorded raw judge output, human
adjudication of disagreements, paired analysis, and uncertainty reporting that
matches the actual sample size.

Until that work is independently reviewed, do not present results as proof of
performance, a ranking of models, or a universal reliability claim.
